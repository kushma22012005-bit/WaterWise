ABOUT WATERWISE

Every Drop Tells a Story.

WaterWise is a water consumption analysis and forecasting project that helps users explore historical usage patterns, understand environmental inputs, and examine predictions generated by a trained forecasting model.

Explore Dashboard →
≈

Our Purpose

Raw consumption records can be difficult to understand. WaterWise presents the available data through summaries, interactive charts, and forecasting tools to make consumption patterns easier to explore.

⌁

Our Approach

The project uses date, time, consumption, temperature, rain, and sunlight information. A trained Random Forest model uses temporal and environmental features to estimate water consumption.
