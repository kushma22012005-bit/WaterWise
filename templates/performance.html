METRIC GUIDE

How to Read the Results

Mean Absolute Error (MAE)

Lower MAE generally means predictions are closer to the actual consumption values on average.

Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)

Lower RMSE generally indicates smaller prediction errors, with larger errors penalized more heavily.

R² Score

A score closer to 1 indicates a stronger fit to the evaluation data. A negative score means the model performs worse than predicting the test-set mean under the standard R² definition.

Evaluation note: These values come from the model's held-out test data. The project uses a chronological train/test split to evaluate performance on later records. Metric values alone do not guarantee reliable future predictions.
